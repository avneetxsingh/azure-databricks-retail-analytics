# Practice dataset provenance

The repository owner supplied these three original project CSVs and confirmed on November 20, 2026 that they are entirely synthetic practice data and may be published in this public repository. The records are unchanged from the supplied files.

- `sales.csv`: 20 transactions from September 1–10, 2026.
- `customers.csv`: 10 synthetic customer records.
- `products.csv`: 10 practice product records.

Source attribution and a separate upstream dataset license were not supplied. The project's MIT license covers its original code and documentation; it should not be interpreted as evidence of ownership of an upstream dataset. Preserve this provenance notice and confirm applicable rights before redistributing the data elsewhere.

Run `python tools/validate_sample_data.py` to check keys, joins, and the documented revenue/unit totals. These examples do not represent a real business or its customers.
